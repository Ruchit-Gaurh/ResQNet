# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Families and citizens reporting missing, found, safe, or sighted people during disasters; volunteers, hospitals, relief camps, and authorized rescuers coordinating under stress and intermittent connectivity; responder administrators verifying identity and operational information.

## Product Purpose

ResQNet is an offline-first disaster identity and reunification network. It preserves reports locally, carries lightweight evidence through nearby phones, reconciles information at a central backend when connectivity returns, and helps authorized responders act without presenting unverified machine suggestions as confirmed facts. Success means a report survives disconnection, can travel device to device, reaches responders with provenance, and contributes to a human-verified reunification.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, decisive. The product should feel like dependable emergency infrastructure, not a consumer social network or speculative technology demonstration.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic startup dashboards, decorative gradients, game-like emergency interfaces, dense technical jargon, celebratory treatment of possible matches, fake live telemetry, and controls that imply GPS or Bluetooth precision the hardware cannot provide.

## Design Principles

1. Put the urgent human action first.
2. State what is known, delayed, relayed, or unverified without ambiguity.
3. Preserve function with partial information and poor connectivity.
4. Reveal technical diagnostics progressively, while keeping responder evidence inspectable.
5. Use sensor guidance as assistance, never as a guarantee of a person's exact position.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use large touch targets, readable system typography, strong contrast, redundant non-colour status cues, keyboard-safe forms, reduced-motion compatibility, simple language, and layouts resilient to small Android screens, long names, and multilingual text.
